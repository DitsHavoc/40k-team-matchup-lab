Import your own game results

Upload UTF-8 CSV games with event_name, event_date, player_a/b, faction_a/b, disposition_a/b and event_format. For singles, either provide the published 20-point scores or both raw_vp_a and raw_vp_b — converted using the official WTC brackets. Inconsistent published and calculated scores are rejected.

Recommended: include source_url, round, team_a, team_b, event_size_teams, event_format, score_type, source_url, verified player profile links, terrain_layout, mission_a, mission_b, rules_version and edition. Duplicate identification requires event, round and both player names.
