Import real games

Upload a UTF-8 CSV with one row per individual match. Case-insensitive faction aliases supported. At least: event_name, event_date, player_a, player_b, faction_a, faction_b, disposition_a, disposition_b, score_a_20, score_b_20.

Recommended: include source_url, round, team_a, team_b, event_size_teams, terrain_layout, terrain_pack, mission_a, mission_b, rules_version and edition. Duplicate identification requires event, round and both player names.

Dataset management

Imported results remain on this device. To share them with other people, export the CSV and commit an approved dataset to GitHub; shared server storage is a separate upgrade.

Never upload private BCP exports without permission. Avoid personal information beyond public player handles.

Data sources to collect

UKTC ITTs, WTC and other 11th-edition major team events (16+ teams). Track each event's provenance and rules version. Unknown maps and dispositions remain blank rather than guessed.
